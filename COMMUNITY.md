## More places to look

- The [OpenAI Games Showcase](https://developers.openai.com/showcase/games) and [OpenAI Website Showcase](https://developers.openai.com/showcase/websites) include multiple model generations. Check the model on the individual project page.
- SandBase's [Astra list](https://github.com/sandbase-lab/awesome-gpt-6-2-astra) and [Sol list](https://github.com/sandbase-lab/awesome-gpt-6-2-sol) collect creator recordings, many of them captured from generated HTML.
- @cheercheung's [Astra examples](https://github.com/cheercheung/awesome-gpt-6-2-astra-guide-and-use-cases) and [Sol examples](https://github.com/cheercheung/awesome-gpt-6-2-sol-use-cases) help trace social posts, recordings, and briefs.
- Model labels in these discovery lists sometimes conflict with creators' posts. For example, [Mallow Catch's creator](https://x.com/RenaRoze_doll/status/2118310499213312478) credits Opus 5.5 despite mentioning GPT-6.2 in the same post. A model mentioned alongside a demo can be unrelated to how it was built.
